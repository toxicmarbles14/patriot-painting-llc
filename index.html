Who We Are

A Strong Foundation. A Beautiful Finish.

Patriot Painting LLC is a fictional, Chicago-inspired painting and drywall company built around one belief: every great finish begins with great preparation.

Our imagined team brings together residential painting, drywall installation, and wall repair to create inviting, polished spaces. From classic Chicago two-flats to modern condominiums, our approach emphasizes detail, communication, and pride in craftsmanship.

We believe a newly painted room should feel like more than a cosmetic update. It should make homeowners excited to enjoy their space again.

Built on Three Principles

Preparation: Addressing surface imperfections before painting begins.

Craftsmanship: Giving attention to edges, finishes, joints, and details.

Respect: Treating each home and project with care.
