Our Expertise

One Team. Complete Wall Solutions.

At the fictional Patriot Painting LLC, we believe painting and drywall services should work together seamlessly. A quality paint finish depends on the condition of the surface beneath it, which is why our imagined company emphasizes preparation before decoration.

Our service concept is designed for Chicago homeowners who want to refresh their living spaces, repair aging walls, or finish a newly remodeled room.

What We Offer

Our Core Services

Interior Painting

Update bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, offices, and hallways with carefully chosen colors and finishes. Preparation includes protecting surrounding surfaces and addressing minor imperfections.

Exterior Painting

Refresh suitable siding, doors, trim, and exterior features. Proper cleaning, preparation, priming, and coating selection help surfaces withstand seasonal exposure.

Drywall Installation

Build smooth new wall and ceiling surfaces for remodeling projects, room conversions, and interior updates. Work includes panel placement, fastening, joint taping, and finishing.

Drywall Repair & Patching

Address damaged wall sections, dents, holes, and cracks. Repairs are blended into existing surfaces and prepared for painting.

Trim and Door Painting

Give baseboards, door frames, interior doors, and decorative trim a clean, coordinated appearance that complements surrounding walls.

Wall Preparation & Priming

Improve paint performance with cleaning, patching, sanding, appropriate caulking, and primers selected for the surface condition.

Our Approach

2. Surface Preparation

3. Installation or Painting

4. Final Inspection

Why Preparation Matters

A freshly painted wall can only look as good as its underlying surface. Uneven drywall joints, dents, cracks, and damaged corners can remain visible beneath paint.

Combining drywall and painting work makes it easier to plan repairs before the final finish.
